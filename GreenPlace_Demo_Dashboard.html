GreenPlace: energy-efficient SDN controller placement

Generated 2026-10-23 17:01 from result files. Green = our methods; grey = baselines.

Honest summary. GreenPlace is not the best static placer (7.4% above the MIP lower bound; NSGA-II 4.2%). It is statistically tied on lifecycle energy, and has 23% fewer switch migrations than NSGA-II (95% CI [-32, -14]%, Holm p <0.001). Against the exact-MIP controller set the advantage is smaller and its mean CI includes zero. Simulation-in-the-loop variants can cut migrations further (at higher energy). Not validated on an emulated or physical testbed.
